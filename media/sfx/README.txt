Drop your own sound files here to replace the generated sounds.
Name each file after the moment it plays (any of .mp3 .wav .ogg .m4a):

  start.wav    lobby -> film transition (leader joined)
  cut.wav      between scenes (not played for "Continue" links)
  freeze.wav   the frame freezes and the choice appears
  dread.wav    LOOPED quietly while the leader is deciding (make it loop cleanly)
  resolve.wav  the choice locks in (the dread loop cuts off right before this)
  ending.wav   the ending screen appears
  reset.wav    back to the QR lobby

Any moment without a file uses the built-in dark rumble/drone sound.
Refresh the /host page after adding files.
